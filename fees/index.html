Fomo Trading Fees Explained (2026)

Fomo's fee structure isn't posted in one obvious place, so here's the plain breakdown, current as of 2026.

Spot trading fees

Roughly 0.5% per trade. Applying a valid referral code takes 10% off that fee — so a 0.5% fee becomes 0.450%. On small trades, absolute fees can feel disproportionate simply because there's often a practical minimum cost per trade regardless of size — worth keeping in mind if you trade frequently in small amounts.

Perpetuals fees (not available to US users)

Perpetuals run through an integration with Hyperliquid. Fomo adds roughly 0.05% per side on top of Hyperliquid's own underlying fees — in practice, this roughly doubles the direct cost of a perp trade compared to trading spot. Perpetuals are not currently available to US-based users.

Other costs to know about

As a non-custodial app, on-chain network fees (gas) apply separately from Fomo's own trading fee and vary by chain (Solana, Base, BNB Chain, Monad) and network conditions — these aren't set by Fomo and aren't discounted by a referral code.
