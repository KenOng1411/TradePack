Fomo is a **non-custodial** trading app, which means it never takes control of your funds — you connect your own
wallet and every trade settles directly on-chain. That single design choice shapes almost everything about how the
app works, so it's worth understanding before you place your first trade.

## 1. Connect a wallet, not an account

There's no email sign-up and no password. Instead, you connect a self-custodied wallet that supports one of Fomo's
four supported chains: **Solana, Base, BNB Chain, and Monad**. Your keys — and your funds — stay with you at every
step; Fomo's app is an interface on top of the chain, not a custodian holding a balance on your behalf.

## 2. Pick your chain

Each supported chain has its own liquidity, token listings, and gas costs. If you already hold assets on Solana or
Base, starting there is usually the path of least friction. Switching chains inside the app doesn't move your
existing balances — you're simply pointing the interface at a different network.

## 3. Enter a referral code before your first trade

If you have one, this is the moment it matters: **new users get 10% off trading fees with a valid referral code**,
and the discount applies automatically once it's entered — no manual calculation needed. Referrers, in turn, earn a
share of the trading fees generated by the users they refer, credited when those users actively trade. See our
[referral code page](/referral-code) for the exact steps.

## 4. Understand the fee before you confirm

Spot trades on Fomo cost roughly **0.5% per order** before any discount. That fee is charged on the trade itself,
separate from network gas costs, which vary by chain and current congestion. Our [fee calculator](/fees) shows the
exact numbers for both a standard and a discounted order.

## 5. Perpetuals — availability depends on where you are

Fomo also offers perpetual futures trading through Hyperliquid. **This is currently not available to users in the
United States.** If you're evaluating Fomo specifically for perps, confirm eligibility in your jurisdiction before
relying on that feature.

## A note on trust

Fomo raised a **$75M Series B led by Index Ventures**, with participation from Union Square Ventures and Benchmark —
a signal of institutional confidence in the non-custodial model. In March 2026, the SEC and CFTC also clarified the
regulatory framework for non-custodial wallet interfaces, introducing a "Covered User Interface Provider" category
that applies to apps structured like Fomo.
