A referral code is the single easiest way to lower your trading costs on Fomo. Here's exactly how it works, with no
guesswork.

## What the discount actually does

New users get **10% off trading fees** with a valid referral code. Since spot trades cost roughly 0.5% per order
before any discount, a valid code brings that down to about 0.45% per order — a saving that compounds with every
trade you make. Try the numbers yourself with our [fee calculator](/fees).

## How referrers earn

Referrers earn a share of the trading fees generated by the users they refer. That share is **credited when those
users actively trade** — it isn't a one-time signup bonus, and it isn't paid for accounts that never trade.

## Applying a code the right way

1. Install the Fomo app and connect a supported wallet (Solana, Base, BNB Chain, or Monad).
2. Enter the referral code where prompted during onboarding, before your first trade.
3. Confirm the discount is reflected in the fee shown before you submit an order.

If you land on this site from a Google ad, our [referral code page](/referral-code) has the code pre-filled along
with the full breakdown above the fold.

## Keep it in perspective

A fee discount doesn't change the underlying risk of trading. Cryptocurrency markets are volatile, and nothing here
